Forelark Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Forelark is an inventory forecasting app for Shopify merchants. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and your rights.

Data we access

When you install Forelark on your Shopify store, the app accesses:

  • Products and variants — SKUs, titles, prices, and unit costs, used to build the inventory dashboard.
  • Inventory levels — on-hand, available, committed, and incoming quantities per location, used to compute stockout dates and reorder suggestions.
  • Orders — line items and order dates, used only to compute sales velocity. We do not read customer names, emails, addresses, or payment information.
  • Locations — names and IDs, used to filter forecasts by location.

How we use the data

The data above is used solely to provide forecasting features: sales velocity, days-to-stockout, reorder points, suggested order quantities, and purchase-order summaries. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data storage

We store only what the app needs to run:

  • Your forecasting settings (lead time, safety stock, target coverage, sales history window, location) and any per-product overrides.
  • An inventory snapshot, refreshed every few hours: product and variant titles, SKUs, prices and unit costs, stock quantities per location, and the number of units of each variant sold per day. It holds no order details, no customer information and no payment data.
  • Any Stocky CSV files you import.

Orders are read through Shopify's bulk export only to count units sold per day; individual orders are not stored.

Data retention and deletion

When you uninstall Forelark, or when Shopify sends a shop/redact request, we delete the store's settings, overrides, inventory snapshot and imported files. You can request a copy of your data at any time using the contact details below.
