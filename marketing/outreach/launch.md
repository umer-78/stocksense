## Announcement draft

Subject: We launched StockSense (unlisted) — a Stocky replacement for Shopify

Stocky, Shopify's native inventory app, shut down on August 31, 2026. We spent
the last few months building StockSense, a forecasting app that fills part of
that gap, and it's now live in unlisted mode on the Shopify App Store.

What it does:
- Sales velocity, days-to-stockout and reorder suggestions for every variant,
  computed from the store's real order history
- Every recommendation shows its math in plain English (velocity, lead-time
  demand, safety stock, reorder point, order quantity)
- Edit lead time, safety stock % or target coverage and the forecast recomputes
  live
- Purchase order builder with CSV export
- Imports Stocky CSV exports (variants + PO history) so merchants keep their
  history
- Per-location forecasting, GDPR-compliant webhooks, built on the Shopify Admin
  API

Honest limits: CSV export only (no PO emailing), no supplier management, one
location at a time, and no AI/ML anywhere — the math is transparent by design.

Pricing: Free (100 SKUs), Growth $19/mo (2,000 SKUs), Pro $49/mo (unlimited).
14-day trials on paid plans.

It's unlisted right now, so it's installable by direct link but not in App
Store search. We're using this phase to fix real-world issues before the public
review queue (30-50 days). If you cover Shopify apps or indie SaaS, we'd love a
mention — and we're happy to share what we learn from the unlisted phase.

## Submission checklist — free directories & newsletters (all verified live)

$0 budget: every entry below is free. All were verified as real and accepting
submissions in September 2026.

**Shopify-specific (highest relevance):**
1. **Ecomgraph** (ecomgraph.com) — indexes new Shopify App Store launches; has
   a "Submit app" page and a monthly digest newsletter. Submit the App Store
   listing URL once the app is live.
2. **MerchantPick** (merchantpick.com) — independent Shopify app directory with
   developer listing pages ("Promote your app"). Free listing; claims it
   indexes live App Store data.
3. **ShopifyAppReviews** (shopifyappreviews.com) — ranked roundups + "Submit
   your app" form. Free.

**General SaaS launch platforms (free tiers):**
4. **Product Hunt** (producthunt.com) — the big one. Free; schedule a launch
   day, prepare a gallery + first comment. Do this after the public listing is
   approved, not during unlisted.
5. **BetaList** (betalist.com) — pre-launch/beta startups; free but the queue
   is 4-6 weeks. Submit early, it may land right as you go public.
6. **SaaSHub** (saashub.com) — free submit; evergreen "alternatives" traffic.
   Good for the "Stocky alternative" search angle.
7. **Uneed** (uneed.com) — free queue (paid skip exists, not needed); daily
   launch competition traffic.
8. **Peerlist Launchpad** (peerlist.io) — free; launches open every Monday.
9. **MicroLaunch** (microlaunch.net) — free tier; month-long launch cycle,
   good for sustained (not one-day) visibility.
10. **Indie Hackers** (indiehackers.com) — free product listing + community
    post; good for the indie-SaaS story.

**Order of operations (free, sequential):**
1. Unlisted live + stable → submit to Ecomgraph, MerchantPick, ShopifyAppReviews
   (they index the App Store listing, so this works even unlisted).
2. Post the announcement to Indie Hackers + relevant newsletters.
3. Submit to BetaList now (long queue).
4. When public review is approved → Product Hunt launch day, then SaaSHub,
   Uneed, Peerlist Launchpad, MicroLaunch.

**Newsletters (free, Shopify-adjacent):** look for indie-SaaS and ecommerce
roundups that accept submissions (e.g. via their "submit" pages or a reply-to
address). Verify each one accepts submissions before sending; drop any that
don't respond or are clearly dead.
